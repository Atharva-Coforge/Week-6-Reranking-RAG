Month-End Close Procedure This writing, mec-us-0001 at version v1.0, tells every business unit of Halden & Co. how a finished month is finalized and how its lines are labeled. Amounts are in USD. It applies to every business unit that keeps financial records on a monthly cycle. It has been in force since 2025-09-01, and no later version replaces it. A close is the calendar month being finalized. A weekday is a day the offices are open for ordinary operations, leaving out weekends and company holidays. A seal is the moment that month's records become fixed. A ledger category is the label a transaction receives so it lands in the chart of accounts. The posting group is the small set of people who may type or release lines while the month is still open.
