Someone in the posting group confirms that everything already known has been recorded. On the fifth weekday after a month turns over, the finance lead signs, and the records for that month are sealed. Nothing already written can be altered after that signature. The finance lead must sign before the seal is applied. Until that signature exists, lines can still be added or fixed. During those same days a team may find that a travel cost or an unpaid bill was given the wrong category. While the signature is still missing, a member of the posting group can correct the category and recheck the balance that moved. After the signature, that path is closed.
