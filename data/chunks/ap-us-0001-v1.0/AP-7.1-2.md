The finance manager answers in writing on the invoice record. Approving payment lets the clerk schedule it. Asking for further information sends the invoice back to the supervisor with the gap named, such as a delivery quantity that is missing or a price that still differs from the purchase order. Sending it back to the vendor includes a short statement of what failed the match. The clerk does not pay the invoice while that choice is still open. If the finance manager is away, the referral waits. The supervisor does not name a substitute approver and does not release the invoice merely to meet the payment period. The hold stays until the decision is on the record. A decision that arrives after the payment period has ended still controls the invoice.
