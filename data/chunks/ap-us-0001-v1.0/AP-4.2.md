AP-4.2 Duplicate payment controls Before payment, the AP clerk confirms that no earlier payment exists against the same invoice number, purchase order, and vendor. The payment system flags a possible duplicate when vendor, amount, and invoice number match a paid record. A flagged invoice is not paid until the AP supervisor confirms it is not a duplicate. A duplicate found after payment is recorded and sent to the finance manager for recovery.
