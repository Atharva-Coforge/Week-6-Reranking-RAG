EXP-6.1 Exception and escalation Where a claim is missing a required slip, goes over the meal cap with no supervisor approval, is late with no supervisor approval, or still lists spirits, beer, or wine, the claim is returned or sent to the supervisor. The supervisor may approve it as filed, approve a smaller amount, or refuse the disputed part. Avery Chen, expense audit lead, records the decision and answers an escalated claim within 5 business days. Nothing is paid until that decision is on the claim.
