EXP-2.1 Definitions A repayable cost is a business cost an employee pays and may claim back under this procedure. A slip is an itemized proof of purchase showing the vendor, the date, and the amount paid. A meal cost is food and non-alcoholic drinks bought for a business purpose, whether the employee is alone or with others. The filing date is the date the employee sends a claim in for review.
