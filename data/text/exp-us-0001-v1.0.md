EXP-1.1 Purpose
This procedure (exp-us-0001, v1.0) governs how Halden & Co. employees submit, review, and receive
repayment of business costs. Amounts are in USD. It applies to every employee who incurs a business cost
while doing the job. It is effective 2025-09-01, and no later version supersedes it.
EXP-2.1 Definitions
A repayable cost is a business cost an employee pays and may claim back under this procedure. A slip is an
itemized proof of purchase showing the vendor, the date, and the amount paid. A meal cost is food and
non-alcoholic drinks bought for a business purpose, whether the employee is alone or with others. The filing
date is the date the employee sends a claim in for review.
EXP-3.1 Submission requirements
An employee files a claim in the expense system and lists each cost by date, amount, and business purpose. A
claim with no itemized business purpose goes back to the employee before review starts.
EXP-3.2 Receipt requirements
An employee attaches a slip for any single cost above $25. A cost of $25 or less may be filed with no slip if the
amount and the business purpose are written on the claim.
EXP-3.3 Required claim form
Every claim is filed on FORM-ER-19. A submission that uses any other template is returned before review.
EXP-4.1 Meal expense caps
A meal cost of $75 or less per meal is repayable when the claim is valid and, where Section EXP-3.2 requires it,
a slip is attached. The cap applies to each meal. It does not limit what the employee may spend on meals across
a full day.
EXP-4.2 Spirits, beer, and wine
Spirits, beer, and wine are refused repayment under this procedure. Where a meal slip lists those items, the
employee removes their price from the amount requested before the claim is filed. A claim that still contains
that price is sent back for correction.

EXP-4.6 Ground transportation
Airport car service is booked only with Northstar Logistics. A ride taken with any other car service is a personal
cost and is not paid back.
EXP-5.1 Submission timing
An employee files a claim within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred. A claim filed after that window
needs supervisor approval before it is processed.
EXP-5.2 Equipment purchases
A single equipment purchase above $250 needs facilities approval before the employee pays. That approval is
noted on the claim.
EXP-6.1 Exception and escalation
Where a claim is missing a required slip, goes over the meal cap with no supervisor approval, is late with no
supervisor approval, or still lists spirits, beer, or wine, the claim is returned or sent to the supervisor. The
supervisor may approve it as filed, approve a smaller amount, or refuse the disputed part. Avery Chen, expense
audit lead, records the decision and answers an escalated claim within 5 business days. Nothing is paid until that
decision is on the claim.
EXP-7.1 Recording and outcome
The amount filed, the amount approved, and any supervisor approval are stored on the original claim.
Deductions, approved excesses, and approved late filings stay with the claim for audit.
EXP-8.1 Staff celebrations
Alcohol served at an internal staff celebration can be submitted for reimbursement from the site social fund.
That request is paid outside the business-meal claim, and it does not follow the meal cap in Section EXP-4.1.
The social fund pays only while a balance remains.
